[JSC] A marking thread pays for the wake-up of another one with marking, and an idle helper leaves the collection

Every wake-up in the marking protocol woke every sleeping helper thread:
each donation of cells to the shared mark stacks, each task of the
constraint solver, each helper that saw the marking terminate, and the
start and the end of the cycle. A collection of a linked structure
donates one or two cells at a time, so it made about 220 thread wake-ups
for a few MB of marking, and on a virtual machine one wake-up costs the
waker more than those cells take to mark.

A wake-up is now paid for. Each time a visitor has visited
minimumBytesMarkedForParallelMarking (now 2 MB), the collection may wake
one more marker: Collector::didPayForMarkerWakeup. A marker that was
woken pays after an eighth of that: a wake-up that gave its marker work
was worth its cost. Collector::choosePaidForMarkersToWake gives the
markers that are paid for and not woken yet, for a donation, for a task
of the constraint solver and when the mutator resumes. The one wake-up
that needs no payment is chooseMarkerToWakeIfNoneIsMarking, for a thread
that stops marking while the mutator runs. Collector::wakeMarkers wakes
with m_markingMutex released.

No helper parks in a collection. The helper pool runs the helper task on
one more thread per request (ParallelHelperClient::setTaskOnRequest,
requestThread), and hears of a cycle when the cycle first asks for a
thread. A helper that finds no shared work and no task leaves, so the
notices that are for the conductor, and the end of the cycle, have no
helper to wake. Before it parks or leaves, a marker that runs dry while
another one is at work looks at the shared mark stacks for
gcMarkerPollMS: that one shares cells within a round or two.

A collection is small until a visitor has visited
minimumBytesMarkedForParallelMarking. Collector::runFixpointPhase keeps
the mutator stopped that long, for every scheduler, unless
minimumGCPauseMS is 0. A collection of collectContinuously is never
small: that option is there to have the marking overlap the mutator.

SlotVisitor::drain gets nothing per cell. It reads the clock, looks at
what the visitor owes and counts the cells that were visited again once
per round, and once per minimumNumberOfScansBetweenRebalance cells in a
chain, where every round has one cell and read the clock. The two checks
of donateKnownParallel that need no lock are as they were.
